## C) Clusters and representatives

Method: each id is assigned to the cluster of its strongest look-alike/read-alike neighbour; where chains would bridge two clusters (e.g. k16 links to k07 visually and to j02 verbally), the id is placed with the closer match and the two representatives were checked to be clearly different both visually and verbally. All adopted representatives were cross-checked pairwise against the B list. JP-origin (j/k) chosen when quality is equal.
